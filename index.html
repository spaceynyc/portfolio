One person, idea to shipped

I’m a creative technologist in New York; online I go by Space. Every project on this page is a solo build: I design it, write the code, and ship it.

My work runs along two lines that usually belong to different people. One is agents that do real work in real interfaces: reading a screen, driving a phone, arguing with each other, or talking a shopper through a catalog. The other is worlds: 3D and audio-reactive spaces where an abstract idea becomes somewhere you can move around.

What I take on

  • Agents that act. Tools that read, decide, and operate real interfaces.

    See SUE, PhoneAgent, Research Lab

  • Interfaces for complex systems. Dashboards and tools that make a messy domain legible.

    See Zipchair Intel, Drift

  • Automation. Pipelines that take repeated work off a person’s or a team’s hands.

    See AEROEDEN, OpenClaw

  • Interactive worlds. 3D, sound, and generative graphics that turn an idea into a place.

    See Socionics Galaxy, inner-system

Usual stack React, TypeScript, Python, Node, FastAPI, Three.js and React Three Fiber, GLSL, OpenAI models, SQLite.
